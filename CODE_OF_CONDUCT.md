# Code of Conduct

VERIDIUM is built by an international team working with businesses and governments. We expect everyone who works in our repositories to:

- Be respectful, professional and constructive, in every language we work in.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect confidentiality: what is private in VERIDIUM stays private, including partner, customer and government information.
- Raise concerns early and openly with a maintainer.

Harassment, discrimination, or disclosure of confidential information is not tolerated. Report concerns privately to the organization owners. They will review every report and may restrict access to the organization.
