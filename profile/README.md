# VERIDIUM Global

**Identity and traceability infrastructure for regulators, customs, laboratories and the businesses they supervise.**

VERIDIUM gives every product a verifiable digital identity, from production through logistics to the point of sale, and connects the businesses that make and move goods with the public authorities that oversee them.

### What we build

| Area | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Platform core** | Companies, roles, onboarding, Country Packs, adapters, audit, identifiers (GS1 and other global standards) |
| **Link** | The shared, versioned contract between VERIDIUM modules |
| **Freight · Warehouse** | Transport, terminals and storage on one traceable chain |
| **POS** | Verification at the cash register, worldwide |
| **Analytics** | Insight for operators, regulators and brands |
| **Connections** | Marketplace for goods and services partners |

### Principles

- **Standards first:** GS1, EPCIS and open identifiers over proprietary formats.
- **Every module works standalone and connected:** products run on their own and plug into the platform through contracts.
- **Government systems only through national operators**, under each country's law.
- **Security and privacy by design.**
